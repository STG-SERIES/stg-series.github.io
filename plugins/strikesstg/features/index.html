Features

Moderation that stays predictable for players and staff — configure words and ban ladders in config.yml.

Chat, signs & books

Detects banned words in player chat, sign text, and written books — not just public messages.

Whole-word matching

Only full words count. Partial matches inside longer strings are ignored so false positives stay low.

Strikes & censor

Each matched word adds one strike. Offending words are replaced with red *** before others see them.

Vanilla title feedback

Players get the familiar on-screen title: You got N strike(s)! — no custom resource pack required.

Ban thresholds

Stack rules like forever, 7d, 12h, or 30m at strike counts you define.

Persistence & logging

Strike history lives in strikes.yml. Important actions are logged to the server console.

Commands

/strikes to review your record, staff can inspect others or reset strikes with the right permissions.

Bypass permission

Trusted staff can hold strikesstg.bypass so moderation tools do not strike operators during testing.

Permissions at a glance

Defaults follow vanilla expectations: players see their own strikes; admins reset and inspect others.

  • strikesstg.strikes — view own
  • strikesstg.strikes.others — view others (OP)
  • strikesstg.reset — clear strikes (OP)
  • strikesstg.bypass — skip detection
  • strikesstg.admin — full access (OP)
