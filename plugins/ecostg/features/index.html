Features

Everything below ships in the main plugin. Toggle pieces in config.yml without ripping out the rest.

Economy & trading

Starting balance, currency naming, /pay, balance commands, money top, OP tools, and per-player economy toggles. Auction house with search; instant sell via /sell using worth.yml (shulkers count contents).

Jobs

One job per player. Deliver items on a schedule, list worker-tagged AH listings with discounts, and track deadlines in /jobinfo. Miss a deadline and face a real-day cooldown.

Homes

Named homes with dialog menus — teleport, rename, delete, and pick preset or custom HEX colors. Defaults are configurable; operators are not capped by the home limit.

ESC / hub menu

Pause-screen button (custom label) and /menu share one hub: homes, auction, sell, TPA, leaderboards, RTP, friends, pay, stats, ender chest, optional GSit sit/lay, OP gamemode, settings last.

Social & travel

Follow-based friends (mutual follow = friends), TPA / TPAHere with privacy rules, random teleport near you inside the world border, and leaderboards for money, playtime, kills, blocks, and more.

Combat tag

Player vs player hits start a visible timer. While active, home, TPA, and RTP are blocked. Operators are never tagged; PvP against an OP only tags the non-OP.

Settings & privacy

In-hub settings for chat filters, notifications, bio, and visuals — including optional money display under the player in-world (not in tab or chat).

Server-friendly

SQLite storage, structured console logging for important actions, feature flags in config, and builds aligned to each supported Paper line.

Every hub button has a command

Players are never locked into dialogs. Auction, sell, homes, friends, RTP, pay, stats, settings, and the rest all work from chat.
