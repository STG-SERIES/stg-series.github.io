Features

Everything runs server-side — tune fights and flattening in plugins/CatFighting/config.yml.

Automatic quarrels

Cats scan on an interval, roll a start chance, and brawl when another eligible cat is in range. Sitting cats can be excluded.

Visual combat

Hisses, hurt sounds, angry particles, and swipes. They close in face-to-face with configurable speed, duration, and cooldown.

No fight damage

Cat-vs-cat fights never apply real damage — pure spectacle for survival worlds.

Who can fight

Toggle tamed cats, same-owner pairs, and kittens independently in config.

Flatten cats

Hit with a shovel or roll over them with a fast minecart. Flattened cats persist across chunk unloads.

Vanilla-friendly flatten

Uses the SCALE attribute and lie-down pose — not paper-thin Y-scale mods require.

Unflatten options

Shovel toggle, sneak + empty-hand right-click, or /catfighting unflatten on the cat you are looking at.

Admin commands

/catfighting reload, flatten, unflatten — aliases /catfight, /cf. Permission: catfighting.admin (OP).

Config highlights

  • enabled — master fight toggle
  • search-range · start-chance
  • duration-ticks · cooldown-ticks
  • flatten.shovel · flatten.minecart
  • flatten.scale — flattened size
